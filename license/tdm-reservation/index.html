Directive (EU) 2019/790 · Article 4(3)

Sovereign Creator TDM Reservation

Pursuant to Article 4(3) of Directive (EU) 2019/790 on copyright and related rights in the Digital Single Market, originZero.Art and all represented independent human creators expressly reserve all rights over texts, visual artworks, photographs, design artifacts, and metadata hosted across originzero.art and sovereign creator subdomains.

Automated scraping, harvesting, text and data mining (TDM), dataset compilation, or ingestion into generative machine-learning models is strictly prohibited without an explicit written bilateral license from the human rightsholder.
