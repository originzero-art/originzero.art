The Challenge

Great work gets lost when the web feels out of reach.

Across studios, workbenches, and quiet rooms, dedicated painters, sculptors, composers, photographers, poets, and artisans craft original works in silence. Yet finding their creations online is often an uphill battle. This is not because the work lacks depth or beauty, but because building a modern digital presence demands specialized technical knowledge, endless maintenance, and time stolen from the craft itself.

Artists should not have to become software administrators, SEO technicians, or social media performers simply to show the world what they create. True craft deserves an uncluttered home where people who care can find it without friction.

Our Approach

We remove technical barriers so your creation speaks for itself.

Transparent Community & Economic Model

originZero.Art gives artists a quiet space to present their work and prices, completely free of sales fees or commissions. Conversations and sales happen privately, directly between artist and collector.

Today, originZero.Art is sustained directly by its founder. As we open up to public donations and grants, community funds will be held transparently through a European non-profit fiscal host (with a public ledger of all income and expenses), while dedicated technical services are billed separately and lawfully under European regulations.

Once community donations, sponsorships, or revenue from dedicated subdomains begin, funds will be shared as follows (note: institutional research and software grants follow their own dedicated budgets):

50%

50%: Shared Across the Community. Distributed among active artists on the platform (excluding the donor) through creator stipends and art commissions. It works much like a network switch broadcast, where a signal reaches every connected port except the one that sent it.

20%

20%: Supporting New Voices. Directed toward welcoming and supporting promising artists and craftspeople, guided by recommendations from the community and reviewed by the project stewards.

30%

30%: Keeping the Lights On. Covers servers, security, domains, and the day-to-day engineering and administration needed to run and improve the platform. Over time, surplus resources may also help people or causes in need.

Suggestions from the community guide how we support new creators; silence or abstention is never read as disagreement.

We want to build together, guided by clear principles, free from division or greed.
